# Privacy Policy for The Winter Arc

**Last Updated**: October 2, 2025

## Introduction

The Winter Arc ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally

The Winter Arc stores all data locally on your device. We collect and store:

- **Arc Configuration**: Your arc name, duration, goals, and start date
- **Progress Data**: Daily goal completion, streaks, and statistics
- **Journal Entries**: Your personal reflections, mood selections, and photos
- **Settings**: Theme preferences, notification settings, and other app configurations

### Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do NOT collect personal identification information
- We do NOT transmit your data to our servers
- We do NOT share your data with third parties
- We do NOT track your activity outside the app
- We do NOT use analytics or advertising services

## How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to:

- Display your arc progress and statistics
- Save your journal entries and reflections
- Schedule local notifications (if enabled)
- Provide data export functionality
- Remember your preferences and settings

## Data Storage

- All data is stored locally on your device using Android's secure storage
- Your data never leaves your device unless YOU explicitly export it
- Photos are stored in your device's local storage
- No cloud backup or synchronization (all local)

## Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

- All data is stored using Android's secure SharedPreferences
- Photos are stored in app-private directories
- No network transmission of personal data
- Data is only accessible by the app

## Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

- **Access**: View all your data within the app
- **Export**: Export your data as JSON, text summary, or journal
- **Delete**: Clear all data using the "Restart Arc" feature
- **Control**: Disable any feature at any time

## Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

### Required Permissions:
- **Storage**: To save photos attached to journal entries
- **Camera**: To take photos for journal entries (optional use)
- **Notifications**: To send daily reminders (if enabled)

### How We Use Permissions:
- **Storage**: Only used to save and retrieve photos you add to journal entries
- **Camera**: Only used when you choose to take a photo for your journal
- **Notifications**: Only used to send local reminders at your chosen time

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

## Third-Party Services

The Winter Arc does NOT use any third-party services for:
- Analytics
- Advertising
- Cloud storage
- User tracking
- Data collection

## Children's Privacy

The Winter Arc does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences and does not require any personal information.

## Data Export and Sharing

- You can export your data at any time using the built-in export features
- Export creates files that YOU control and can share
- Sharing exported data is entirely your choice
- We recommend protecting exported data as it contains your personal reflections

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Providing notice within the app
- Posting the new policy on our website (if applicable)

## Consent

By using The Winter Arc, you consent to this Privacy Policy.

## Data Retention

- Data is retained on your device until you choose to delete it
- You can delete specific journal entries
- You can restart your arc (deletes all data)
- Uninstalling the app removes all stored data

## User Control

You have complete control:
- All data stored locally
- No account required
- No data collection
- Full export capability
- Complete deletion option

**Summary**: Your privacy matters. The Winter Arc keeps everything local, secure, and under your control. Your data is yours and yours alone.
